Cooking Times and Servings

  • Prep Time : 10 minutes
  • Cook Time : 6 hours
  • Total Time : 6 hr 10 minutes
  • Servings : 8

Reasons to love this recipe

  1. Pork tenderloin slow-cooks with root beer and barbecue sauce until tender and flavorful.
  2. Home cooks have great success with other cuts of pork, like shoulder and butt.
  3. Allrecipes member Crystal S says, "This is a GREAT recipe for a sweet southern type pork."

Ingredients

  • 1 (2-pound) pork tenderloin
  • 1 (12-fluid ounce) can or bottle root beer
  • 1 (18-ounce) bottle your favorite barbecue sauce
  • 8 hamburger buns, split and lightly toasted

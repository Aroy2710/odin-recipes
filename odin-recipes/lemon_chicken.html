Cooking Times and Servings

  • Prep Time : 10 minutes
  • Cook Time : 50
  • Total Time : 1hr
  • Servings : 4

Reasons to love this recipe

  1. This recipe combines lemon, garlic, oregano, and rosemary for Classic Greek flavors.
  2. Home cooks love the ease of using just one pan and an easy-to-follow method for rich results.
  3. Allrecipes member RosyBoba4560 says, “...easy to follow while also being easy to experiment.”

Ingredients

  • 4 pounds skin-on, bone-in chicken thighs
  • 3 medium russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • ½ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ½ cup Greek olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 cup chicken broth, divided
  • 1 teaspoon chopped fresh oregano, or to taste
